Welcome to the Resources for CMP3103 - Autonomous Mobile Robots

Dive into the fascinating world of Autonomous Mobile Robotics! This hands-on module bridges theory and practice, teaching you how to bring robots to life. You'll learn everything from foundational control systems to advanced navigation techniques that allow robots to understand and move through real-world spaces. In lectures, you'll explore the core principles of robotic sensing and control, from navigation to interaction. In the workshops, where you'll put these concepts into action. Through practical programming exercises, you'll experience firsthand how to transform a collection of sensors, motors, and code into an intelligent, moving system that can interact with its environment. Whether you're interested in developing delivery robots, autonomous vehicles, or exploration robots, this module gives you the essential building blocks to understand and create autonomous mobile systems that can navigate our complex world.
